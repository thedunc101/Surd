## Why the text is not here

Three reasons, and none of them is that the chapter is unavailable.

**The repository's rule is no prose of the book.** The transcripts in [`logs/`](logs/)
have chapter prose stripped out of them, and every removal carries its word count and a
hash so the redaction can be checked. Publishing a whole chapter after that would make
the rest of the redaction look arbitrary.

**The refusal record already withholds for this reason.** One task title on the worst day
is held back there with the note that, read cold, it scans as an instruction, which is
the whole problem that record is about. The same judgement applies to a chapter, more so.

**The argument here is structural.** What this archive shows is a classifier stopping
legitimate work 197 times over seven days, and what the author did to carry on. Putting
the text in turns that into an argument about the text, which is a different argument and
not the one the record was built to make.
