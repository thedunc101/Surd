**C. Chronology, intervals, ages and arithmetic**

The port condition is internally defective [Ch 7 paras 13–14]. Furthermore, under the stated upper bound, increasing equipment requires sufficient staffing to preserve the ratio. It does not explain a falling roster. The recovery-count passage changes the scope of its total [Ch 7 paras 17–18].
